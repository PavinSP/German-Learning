**Word order (Q41–44): 2/4.**
- Q41 correct, and both variants offered (*Heute esse ich* / *Ich esse heute*).
- **Q42 — weil failed again, and on the opposite half from last time.**
  Gave *"Weil bin ich müde"* (verb-second inside the weil-clause) while the
  main clause *bleibe ich* was correct. On 09-11 it was the reverse. **The two

- **Q43 — nicht placement with a definite object.** Gave *"Ich sehe nicht den

  ("nicht den Hund, sondern die Katze"). The plain negation wanted here is
  *Ich sehe den Hund nicht*.

## nicht placement re-taught in full

At the learner's request, a fuller explanation than the original Q18 entry —
specifically the framing that **nicht pushes as far right as it can, and a
definite object gets out of its way**, because the object is established
information and not the thing being negated. Contrasted explicitly with the
contrastive reading (which is what was actually produced), and with the
indefinite case where *kein* replaces the article entirely rather than *nicht*
appearing at all.

A 7-question drill (Q46–52) covering definite objects, indefinite objects,
modal + infinitive, separable verbs, adjectives and definite plurals was
issued but **not attempted** — learner stopped for the night. Carry forward.
