## Complete Notes created, then rewritten

Built [Complete Notes.md](../Complete%20Notes.md) as a single exhaustive study
document, assembled from GRAMMAR.md, VOCABULARY.md, all 23 ANSWERS entries,
~70 ERROR_LOG entries, PRONOUN_TABLE.md and STUDY_PLAN.md.

**The learner then made a fair criticism: the first draft read as a log, not
as notes.** It had inherited the trackers' voice — dates, frequency counts,
"recorded 3×", "RESOLVED 09-15" — which documents what happened rather than
teaching anything.

Rewritten throughout. The error material was kept, since it is the most
valuable part, but reframed from *"here is what you got wrong on this date"*
to *"here is the trap and why it catches people"*. Part IX is now organised by
the **cause** of a mistake — English interference, over-applying a real rule,
plausible-but-false shortcuts, small forms that vanish under concentration,
words that sit too close together — which is more useful for revision than a
chronological list, because each cause needs a different fix.

Explanations were added where the first draft only asserted: why dative-only
verbs feel wrong to an English ear, why *kein den Hund* is structurally
impossible rather than merely wrong, why fronting changes emphasis.

## LaTeX/PDF build added

`build_notes.sh` regenerates both `Complete Notes.tex` and `Complete Notes.pdf`
from the Markdown via pandoc + xelatex. Generated rather than hand-maintained
deliberately — keeping two copies of a 1000-line document in sync by hand
guarantees they drift.

Two build problems were found and fixed rather than shipped:
- An `Apple Color Emoji` font declaration aborted the build outright.
- **Palatino has no glyph for → or ✓/✗**, so those were being *silently
  dropped* from the PDF — the build "succeeded" while deleting content. This
  matters because arrows carry meaning throughout (der→den→dem, es→ihm).
  Substituted in the pipeline; the check marks were fixed in the source
  instead, since they were used two different ways.

**New hard rule:** every tracker update must also refresh Complete Notes and
rebuild the PDF, all pushed together. Recorded in CURRENT_STATE.md and
persistent memory.
