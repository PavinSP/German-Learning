# 2026-09-03, continued — House & rooms vocabulary

**Focus:** New vocabulary (learner chose vocabulary over new grammar)

## New vocabulary (7 words)

das Zimmer, die Küche, das Schlafzimmer, das Bad, der Tisch, der Stuhl, das Fenster

Deliberately chosen to include two der-words (Tisch, Stuhl) so the set doubles as
accusative practice, and two nouns with unchanged plurals (Zimmer, Fenster).

Also mentioned in passing: **compound nouns** — Schlafzimmer = schlaf(en) + Zimmer,
with the FINAL part deciding the gender. Not taught as a formal rule, just flagged
as a pattern; learner applied it correctly first try (mein Schlafzimmer).

## Main exercise — 5 sentences (4/5 clean)

1. "Die Küche ist warm." ✅
2. "Ich habe einen Tisch." ✅ accusative der-word
3. "Mein Schlafzimmer ist kalt." ✅ das-word gender agreement + compound noun
4. "Heute sehe ich den Stühl." — grammar fully correct (verb-second ✅, accusative
   den ✅); only the noun spelling was off: Stuhl has no umlaut in the singular
   (die Stühle is the plural). Logged as the third invented-umlaut instance.
5. "Wir haben kein Fenster." ✅ kein with a das-word (not keinen)

## Confirmation round — 3 sentences (2/3, plus one article slip)

1. "Ich sehe den Stuhl." — Stuhl spelling now correct ✅, but the prompt asked for
   "a chair" → einen Stuhl. Definite/indefinite mismatch, not a case error.
2. "Wir haben zwei Tische." ✅ — notably closes out the old "zweinen Hunde" error
   (2026-09-01): number takes no ending and no article, plural formed correctly.
3. "Das Bad ist nicht warm." ✅

## Targeted a/the check — 2 sentences (2/2 clean)

1. "Ich sehe ein Fenster." ✅ indefinite, das-word unchanged in accusative
2. "Ich sehe den Tisch nicht." ✅ definite + nicht at the end — the Q8 rule applied

Confirms the a/the slip was inattention, not a gap. Worth flagging to the learner
that this distinction is the exact trigger for kein vs nicht, so precision there
