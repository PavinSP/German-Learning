## Re-teaching (slower, explicit this time)

- **Verb-second, restated plainly:** [Position 1: anything] — [Position 2: VERB] —
  [Position 3: subject, if not already position 1] — [rest]. Whatever leads the
  sentence "uses" position 1; the verb always claims position 2.
- **schlafen, full conjugation restated:** schlafe/schläfst/schläft/schlafen/
  schlaft/schlafen — schläft is already complete, nothing added.
- **mein/meine test:** always ask "der or die?" first — der Vater→mein,
  die Mutter→meine, every time, no shortcuts.
