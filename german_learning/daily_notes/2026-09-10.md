### New concept surfaced: dative-only verbs (helfen, danken, gehören)

While retesting, asked to complete "Ich helfe ___ (her)" → answered
**"Ich helfe sie"** ❌ → should be **"Ich helfe ihr."** helfen (and danken,
gehören, gefallen) take ONLY a dative object, never accusative, even
though there's just one object — English "I help her" looks like a direct
object but German treats the helped person as a recipient. Immediate
correct retest: "Ich danke ihm" ✅, "Das Buch gehört mir" ✅ — the latter
using a verb (gehören) never pre-taught; learner correctly inferred both
meaning and case from context, though this was flagged as lucky rather than
something to rely on. Self-correction note logged: I used gehören without
explaining its meaning first, which the learner caught — should always
give a new verb's meaning before testing with it.
