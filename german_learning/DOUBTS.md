**Q32 — why "mit dem Kind" is dative**
Learner's reasoning was "isn't that a direct object?" — answered briefly in
session: no, mit is a PREPOSITION and German prepositions govern a case
independently of object roles. mit always takes dative. So dative arises from two
separate causes: (1) indirect object / receiver, (2) after a dative preposition.
Deferred: the full list of dative prepositions (mit, nach, bei, von, zu, aus,
seit) and practice distinguishing the two causes.
