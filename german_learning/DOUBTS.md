**Q32 — why "mit dem Kind" is dative**
Answered briefly in session: mit is a PREPOSITION and German prepositions govern
a case independently of object roles. mit always takes dative. So dative arises
from two separate causes: (1) indirect object / receiver, (2) after a dative
preposition. **Still deferred:** the full list of dative prepositions (mit, nach,
bei, von, zu, aus, seit) and practice distinguishing the two causes.
