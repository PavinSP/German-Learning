### 2026-08-29 — Q1: "She is very nice" — sie ist sehr nett vs Sie sind sehr nett?

These aren't formal/informal versions of the same sentence — they're two different
meanings. sie (lowercase) = "she" → *Sie ist sehr nett* (capitalized only because
it's the start of the sentence). Sie (formal "you", always capitalized, always
takes *sind*) = *Sie sind sehr nett* = "You (formal) are very nice." Spelled the
same, disambiguated by capitalization mid-sentence and by context.

### 2026-08-29 — Q2: Does "ihr" always mean informal "you all"?

Yes. *ihr* = informal plural "you" (friends, family, kids) — never formal.
Formal "you", singular or plural, is always *Sie* + *sind*.
